Free GIF Maker & Video to GIF Converter – private, in your browser

Make a GIF from any video or edit an existing GIF – right here in your browser. Cut a clip out of an MP4 or WebM video, then trim, crop, resize, speed up or reverse it, add text, images and drawings, and animate them with keyframes. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed: your files never leave your device.

How it works – quick guide & FAQ

How to make a GIF from a video

  1. Open the Video to GIF tab and choose your video file (MP4 or WebM; even files of several GB work).
  2. Select the part you want with the timeline, or type the start and end times.
  3. Choose the output width, frames per second (FPS) and dithering, then generate the GIF.
  4. Download the GIF, or open it in the GIF editor to add text, images and animations.

What you can do in the GIF editor

  • Load a GIF, image or video by file, URL or drag and drop.
  • Trim frames, change the speed, play in reverse, set the FPS (up to 50), resize and crop.
  • Add text, image and drawing layers and animate them with keyframes: fade in, fly in, pulse, wiggle, spin or bounce.
  • Export with a transparent background and Floyd–Steinberg dithering for smoother colours.
  • Save your project in the browser or as a project file and continue later.

Frequently asked questions

Is OrcaGif really free, and do I need an account?

Yes. OrcaGif is a free personal project: no sign-up, no account and no cookies.

Are my videos and GIFs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Which video formats can I convert to GIF?

MP4 (H.264) and WebM work in every modern browser. MKV and HEVC (H.265) depend on your browser and device; if a video will not play, convert it to MP4 (H.264) first, for example with HandBrake.

Can I make a GIF from a very large video?

Yes. Only the clip you select is read, so even files of several GB work. Keep the tab in the foreground while the GIF is being generated.
